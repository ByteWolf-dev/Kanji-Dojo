About Kanji Dojo 漢字道場

Kanji Dojo is a trainer for hiragana, katakana and the JLPT N5 kanji. Reviews are scheduled with spaced repetition (SM-2, the same idea Anki uses), and you type romaji which is turned into kana while you type.

Belts

Every belt unlocks new characters: white is hiragana, yellow is katakana, green and blue are the N5 kanji. Correct reviews raise your score, and once it is high enough you can challenge the boss. Get 8 out of 10 right to earn the next belt.

Streaks

Review at least once a day to keep your streak going.
